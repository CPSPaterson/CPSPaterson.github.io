About the CPSP Labs research bot

If you found this page from a user agent in your logs, this explains what made the request and how to make it stop.

What it is

A small, privately run research tool. It reads public questions and discussions to learn which electronic products people are asking for and cannot find. It identifies itself as:

cpsp-labs-research-bot/0.1 (+https://cpsp-labs.com/bot.html)

What it does

  • Reads public posts through each site's official API, a few requests a minute at most, on a daily schedule.
  • Follows robots.txt and each site's terms. It stops and stays off a site that returns 403 or asks it to slow down repeatedly.
  • Never logs in, posts, votes, messages anyone, or tries to get past a CAPTCHA or block.

What it keeps

  • The text of public posts and a link back to each one, in a private database, for analysis by one person.
  • No usernames: authors are stored only as a one-way hash, to count how many different people raised a topic.
  • Nothing is republished, sold, or used to train a model.

Contact or opt out

To have a site or your content excluded, or to report a problem, open an issue at github.com/CPSPaterson/CPSPaterson.github.io/issues. Requests are acted on promptly.
